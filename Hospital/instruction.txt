Key points:

Keep public/ as the only web-accessible folder. All app logic lives outside public/.

app/Core/Router.php handles parsing the URL (/patients/view/1) and instantiates controllers.

app/Core/Middleware.php will house RBAC checks (see Phase 2 plan).

views/layouts/main.php contains the Bootstrap 5 header/sidebar and yields content; include a top nav and sidebar with links conditional on role.
